Design system

How Synsei looks, and why.

Synsei is SYN, synergy, plus SEI, Seek, Experiment, Improve. The name is the method: find something, try it, share what worked and get better. Everything on this page follows from that. No one-off styling. New components get spec'd here before they ship on a second page.

Taglines: "Learning out loud." for the hero, and "Try it. Write it down. Share what worked." for the notebook.

Logo

The Stone. A Teacher's Red stone blob with a white S and two white echo curves, the voice going out. The wordmark is Patua One: "syn" in Teacher's Red, "sei" in Ink Black, red dot on the i.

Colors

Six colors, plus two darker twins for text. Bright Ochre, Sage, and Stone are shapes and accents only, never body text. The dark twins carry words.

Typography

Patua One for headings and the wordmark, its only weight. Nunito Sans for body. Small labels stay in Nunito Sans, never set 13px text in Patua One.

Spacing and tokens

The numbers every page shares.

  • Container — max-width 1080px, padding 0 20px
  • Section — 56px vertical, hero 72px top, 64px bottom
  • Radius — 10px cards, 6px buttons
  • Card grid — 3 columns, gap 20px, 1 column on small screens
  • H1 — Patua One, 44px, line-height 1.15, max-width 800px
  • H2 — Patua One, 32px, margin-bottom 12px
  • Intro — muted, max-width 640px, margin-bottom 32px
  • Kicker — Teacher's Red, bold, 13px, letter-spacing .08em, uppercase
  • Muted text — Ink Black at 68% opacity, 6.07:1, passes AA
  • Section tint — Stone at 8% opacity over Rice Paper
