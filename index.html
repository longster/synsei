How I prompt any AI

Most bad AI answers start with a bad prompt. Mine has four parts: context, task, constraints, format. The same four parts work in Muse, ChatGPT, Copilot, or Claude. The tool changes. The thinking does not.

Nov 12, 2026

Write before you perform

Every story I tell starts on paper: setup, tension, turn, one line each. Writing first keeps me honest about what the story is actually about. Then I say it out loud. Never the other way around.

Nov 12, 2026

Accessibility is built in, not bolted on

If you add accessibility at the end, it shows. Contrast fixes, keyboard paths, clear language, these are design decisions, not a checklist you run the week before launch. Build them in from the first sketch and the product is better for everyone.
